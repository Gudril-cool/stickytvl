The token launch radar that prices patience. TVL that stays is worth more than TVL that flashes.

How StickyTVL prices a launch

The snapshot carries only one change window (change_7d). Durability is therefore a single-window stickiness proxy — the smaller a protocol’s weekly TVL swing, the stickier its TVL is judged — not a cross-window consistency check. Protocols are said to record no token, never to have none: the snapshot’s record is not proof.
